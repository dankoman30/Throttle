Why no solder-bridge rework

SB16 / SB18 · board stays factory-unmodified

This Nucleo-32 keeps its SB16 (PA5↔PB7) and SB18 (PA6↔PB6) solder bridges intact — a bridge only causes a conflict when both paired pins are actively driven at once. PA5 (servo) and PA6 (battery sense) are both used here, but neither PB7 nor PB6 is used anywhere in this pin plan, so leaving them genuinely unused sidesteps the whole issue without touching the board.

Power rails

3V3 · 5V · VIN

⚡ 3V3 for logic and every LED rail on this board. 5V is reserved for the servo specifically, sourced from USB through the ST-Link while bench-powered. VIN is left unconnected — it's an input for external power, not in use here.
